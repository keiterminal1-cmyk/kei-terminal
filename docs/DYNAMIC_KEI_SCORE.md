# Dynamic KEI Score v3.2

KEI Score is calculated from:

- Development: 24%
- Adoption: 20%
- Live Liquidity: 22%
- Community: 12%
- Fundamentals: 12%
- Live Momentum: 10%

Risk penalty:
- High risk reduces score
- Low risk slightly improves score

Live Liquidity:
- Market cap score
- Volume score

Live Momentum:
- 24h price change

Opportunity Score:
KEI 45% + Momentum 35% + Safety 20%

Safety = 100 - Dynamic Risk
